Getting started

Install it in about 30 seconds

Shotglow isn't on the Chrome Web Store, so you load it yourself. It's the same "Load unpacked" flow developers use every day — four clicks, no account, no sign-up.

  1. Download and unzip

    Use the button above, then unzip shotglow.zip. Keep the folder somewhere permanent — Chrome loads the extension from it every time it starts.

  2. Open the extensions page

    Paste chrome://extensions into your address bar and hit Enter.

  3. Turn on Developer mode

    It's the toggle in the top-right corner of that page.

  4. Click "Load unpacked"

    Select the unzipped shotglow folder — the one containing manifest.json. That's it.

Updating later: download the newest zip, unzip it over the old folder, then click the refresh icon next to Shotglow on chrome://extensions.

What it does

Redact it, then make it look good

Hiding the secrets is the point — but a screenshot you're about to post deserves to look deliberate, not dashed off.

Mosaic redaction

Four strength levels, from lightly scrambled to unrecoverable. The pixels are actually destroyed — not covered by a box someone can move.

Frame and shadow

Margin, image scale, corner roundness and a drop shadow — the treatment that makes a screenshot look composed instead of cropped.

Backgrounds

Solid colors, eight gradient presets or a custom two-stop gradient, plus bundled mesh wallpapers — with an optional dot, grid or crosshatch overlay.

Aspect ratios

Export at Auto, 1:1, 4:3 or 16:9. The frame expands to fit the ratio, so your image is never cropped to make it fit.

Copy or download

One click puts the finished PNG on your clipboard, ready to paste into Slack, a doc or an issue. Or save it straight to disk.

Stays on your machine

Everything runs locally in the browser. No account, no upload, no server — there's nowhere for your screenshot to go.
